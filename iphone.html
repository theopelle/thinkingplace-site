On iPhone

The Thinking Place is on the App Store, free, with a daily reminder if you like.

Get it on the App Store

Or keep it on your home screen

If you'd rather not install anything, the web version works just as well from your home screen. It takes ten seconds:

  1. Open the web app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (at the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The Thinking Place now opens from its own icon, full screen, like any other app, and works without a connection.

Good to know

Open it from the home-screen icon rather than from a Safari tab: that way your notes are kept safely, even when Safari clears old website data.

Your notes stay on your iPhone. Export them now and then from Settings to keep a copy.

Daily reminders are only in the app. Moving from the web version to the app? Your notes don't move by themselves: in the web version, open Settings and tap export, then in the app, tap import and choose that file.
