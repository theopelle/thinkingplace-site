Questions

What is The Thinking Place?

A free daily-philosophy app. Every day it shows one short passage from the great thinkers of East and West, with a short note that gives it its context and a question to help you think it through. It takes a minute to read; the thinking can take as long as you like.

Is it really free? Are there ads?

Yes, it's free, with no ads, no subscription, and nothing to buy. There is no account and no tracking either.

Is it a meditation app?

No. It's a beginner's guide to philosophy: a thought to read and a question to sit with. There's a quiet "sit and think" timer with soft sounds if you'd like a few minutes to reflect, but the point is to think, not to empty your mind.

Which philosophies and thinkers will I read?

More than 700 thoughts from twelve paths: Stoicism, Epicureanism, Classical Greek Philosophy, Enlightenment Ethics, Existential Thought, Humanists and Essayists, Buddhist Philosophy, Indian Philosophy, Taoism, Confucianism, Japanese Aesthetics, and Persian and Sufi Poetry. You'll meet Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epicurus, Plato, Kant, Nietzsche, Montaigne, Laozi, Confucius, Kenkō, and Rumi, among many others, and passages from the Dhammapada and the Upanishads. Underlined words open a short explanation of a name or an idea.

Where do the quotes come from?

Every quotation comes from a public-domain text or translation, and each one is checked word for word against its source. The source is credited under every thought, with a link to read the full text. A few older translations are lightly modernised ("thou" becomes "you"), and a few French passages are our own translations; both are noted under the thought.

Do I need an account? Where are my notes kept?

No account. What you write stays only on your device: it is never sent to us or to anyone else, and no one can read it.

What happens to my notes if I clear my browser or change phones?

Because there is no copy anywhere else, clearing your browser's data, uninstalling the app, or losing your phone also erases your notes. Use export in the app's Settings now and then to keep your own copy; to move to a new phone or browser, export there and import the file in the new one. The app can remind you to export. On iPhone, add it to your home screen: in a normal Safari tab, your notes may be erased if you don't visit for a week.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once it has opened once, it works without a connection, in the Android app, on the iPhone home screen, and in the browser.

How do I get it?

On a computer, read it in your browser, nothing to install. On Android, get it on Google Play, free. On iPhone, the app is coming soon to the App Store (it's currently in review by Apple); until then, open it in Safari and add it to your home screen: it opens like an app, full screen.

Does it send reminders?

The Android app can remind you each day, at a time you choose, with a few words about the day's thought. The browser and iPhone versions have no reminders for now.
