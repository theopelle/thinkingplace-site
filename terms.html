Terms & privacy

Your notes are yours alone

Everything you write in The Thinking Place is stored only on this device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account and no server copy, so no one, not even the people who run The Thinking Place, can read, recover or restore your notes.

This also means that if you clear this browser's data, uninstall the app or lose the device, your notes are gone. Use export in the app's Settings to keep your own copy.

What we collect

Nothing. The Thinking Place uses no analytics, no cookies and no tracking, and we don't count our readers. Your name (if you give one), theme, sit-and-think and reminder settings are stored on your device alongside your notes. If you write to us or use the feedback form, we only see what you choose to send. The feedback form is a Google Form, so what you send there is also kept by Google under its own privacy policy; it asks for no name or email unless you choose to add one. Once loaded, the app works entirely offline.

The texts

Every quotation comes from a public-domain text or translation, credited under each thought with a link to its source. Where an old translation has been lightly modernised (archaic forms such as "thou" or a translator's brackets updated, the meaning unchanged), the credit says so. A few passages are our own translations from the French originals, also credited. The short notes and reflection questions are our own. The Thinking Place presents these paths side by side for personal reflection; it does not endorse or rank any of them.

Not advice

The Thinking Place is a space for thought, not a substitute for medical or psychological care. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional.
