The places

Konya

Rumi · Persian and Sufi Poetry

Rumi settled in Konya, in present-day Turkey, as a young man and lived here until his death in 1273. His tomb is now the Mevlana Museum.

Nishapur and Shiraz

Nishapur

Omar Khayyam · Persian and Sufi Poetry

Khayyam was born in this city in north-eastern Iran, and his mausoleum stands here. He worked as a mathematician and astronomer, and helped design a new, very precise calendar.

Shiraz

Sa'di and Hafiz · Persian and Sufi Poetry

Both poets were born and buried in Shiraz, in south-western Iran: Sa'di in the 13th century, Hafiz in the 14th.

Japan

Sei Shōnagon, Kamo no Chōmei, Kenkō and Okakura Kakuzō · Japanese Aesthetics

Sei Shōnagon wrote The Pillow Book at the imperial court in Kyoto around 1000. Chōmei and Kenkō wrote in and around Kyoto in the 13th and 14th centuries. Okakura wrote The Book of Tea in English, published in New York in 1906.

China

Confucius and Mencius · Confucianism

Laozi and Zhuangzi · Taoism

Tradition places Confucius in the state of Lu, Laozi in Chu and Zhuangzi in Song, neighbouring states of ancient China, between the 6th and 4th centuries BCE. Mencius came from Zou, next to Lu.

Baghdad

Al-Ghazali · Persian and Sufi Poetry

Al-Ghazali taught at the Nizamiyya college here from 1091, until a spiritual crisis led him to leave in 1095. Baghdad was also an early centre of Sufism.

Northern India and Kolkata

Northern India

The Buddha · Buddhist Philosophy

The Upanishads and the Bhagavad Gita · Indian Philosophy The Buddha taught across the Ganges plain around the 5th century BCE. The oldest Upanishads were composed in the same region in the centuries before him.

Kolkata

Rabindranath Tagore and Swami Vivekananda · Indian Philosophy

Both were born here, in what was then Calcutta: Tagore in 1861, Vivekananda in 1863.
