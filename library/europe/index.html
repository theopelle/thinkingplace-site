Europe and the Mediterranean, closer

From Athens and Rome to Paris and London

Each dot marks where our thinkers lived and wrote. Tap one to read about the place below.

The places

St Petersburg

Fyodor Dostoevsky · Existential Thought

Edinburgh

David Hume and Adam Smith · Enlightenment Ethics

Hume was born here in 1711 and became a central figure of the Scottish Enlightenment. Adam Smith spent his last years in the city, at Panmure House in the Canongate.

Königsberg

Immanuel Kant · Enlightenment Ethics

Yasnaya Polyana

Leo Tolstoy · Existential Thought

Tolstoy's family estate south of Moscow, where he was born in 1828 and wrote War and Peace and Anna Karenina. He is buried on the estate.

Amsterdam and Rotterdam

Amsterdam

Baruch Spinoza and René Descartes · Enlightenment Ethics

Spinoza was born here in 1632. Descartes lived in the Dutch Republic for about twenty years and published his Discourse on Method in Leiden in 1637.

Rotterdam

Erasmus · Humanists and Essayists

Erasmus was born here around 1466. He lived and taught across Europe, from Paris and Cambridge to Basel, where he died in 1536.

London, Malmesbury and Wrington

London

John Stuart Mill and Mary Wollstonecraft · Enlightenment Ethics

George Eliot and Francis Bacon · Humanists and Essayists Mill was born here in 1806. Wollstonecraft lived and wrote here, and published A Vindication of the Rights of Woman in 1792. George Eliot also lived and wrote in London. Francis Bacon was born here in 1561.

Malmesbury and Wrington

Thomas Hobbes and John Locke · Enlightenment Ethics

Hobbes was born in Malmesbury, Wiltshire, in 1588, and Locke in Wrington, Somerset, in 1632. Hobbes wrote Leviathan in exile in Paris; Locke spent years in exile in the Dutch Republic from 1683.

Frankfurt

Arthur Schopenhauer · Existential Thought

Schopenhauer lived here from 1833 until his death in 1860.

Paris

Blaise Pascal and Simone Weil · Existential Thought

Émilie du Châtelet, Voltaire and Denis Diderot · Enlightenment Ethics

François de La Rochefoucauld, Jean de La Bruyère and Nicolas Chamfort · Humanists and Essayists Pascal's notes were published after his death as the Pensées. Du Châtelet was born here in 1706 and Voltaire in 1694; Diderot edited the Encyclopédie here. La Rochefoucauld's Maxims came out here in 1665, La Bruyère was born here in 1645, and Chamfort died here in 1794. Simone Weil was born here in 1909.

Switzerland and Geneva

Switzerland

Friedrich Nietzsche · Existential Thought

Geneva

Jean-Jacques Rousseau and Voltaire · Enlightenment Ethics

Rousseau was born here in 1712. Voltaire spent most of his last twenty years at Ferney, just outside the city.

Bordeaux

Michel de Montaigne · Humanists and Essayists

Montaigne wrote his Essays at his château near Bordeaux and served as the city's mayor from 1581 to 1585.

Florence and Arezzo

Florence

Niccolò Machiavelli · Humanists and Essayists

Machiavelli was born here in 1469 and served the Florentine Republic from 1498 to 1512. Removed from office when the Medici returned, he wrote The Prince on his farm near San Casciano, outside the city.

Arezzo

Petrarch · Humanists and Essayists

Petrarch was born here in 1304. He spent much of his life in Avignon and nearby Vaucluse, and his last years in Arquà, near Padua.

Aix-en-Provence

Vauvenargues · Humanists and Essayists

Vauvenargues was born here in 1715. After years as a soldier he settled in Paris, where he died in 1747.

Rome

Seneca, Marcus Aurelius and Boethius · Stoicism

Lucretius · Epicureanism

Epictetus also lived here, enslaved and later freed, until his banishment to Greece. Boethius, a Roman senator, wrote The Consolation of Philosophy in prison while awaiting execution in 524.

Murcia

Ibn Arabi · Persian and Sufi Poetry

Ibn Arabi was born here, in Muslim Spain, in 1165. He travelled across North Africa and the Middle East and died in Damascus in 1240.

Athens

Socrates, Plato and Aristotle · Classical Greek Philosophy

Epicurus · Epicureanism

Zeno, the founder of Stoicism Three schools began in this one city within a century. Epictetus later taught at Nicopolis, in north-western Greece.

Ephesus

Heraclitus · Classical Greek Philosophy

A Greek port on the coast of present-day Turkey, where Heraclitus taught around 500 BCE that everything flows.

Konya

Rumi · Persian and Sufi Poetry

Rumi settled in Konya, in present-day Turkey, as a young man and lived here until his death in 1273. His tomb is now the Mevlana Museum.

Baghdad

Al-Ghazali · Persian and Sufi Poetry

Al-Ghazali taught at the Nizamiyya college here from 1091, until a spiritual crisis led him to leave in 1095. Baghdad was also an early centre of Sufism.
