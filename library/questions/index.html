Questions for good conversations

A good question can carry a whole evening. Each one here grew from a thought in the app, and needs no quote to work. Choose by who you'll be with, or let us pick one for you.

With friends

  • What's a good day from long ago that you still think about?

    from EpicurusEpicureanism

  • What upset you once that you can laugh about now?

    from Marcus AureliusStoicism

  • Could you spend a whole day alone, with no phone, and enjoy it?

    from Arthur SchopenhauerExistential Thought

  • Would you take a pill that made you happy forever?

    from MillEnlightenment Ethics

  • Do your best friends make you more yourself, or more like them?

    from AristotleClassical Greek Philosophy

  • Would you rather have a leader who is loved or one who is feared?

    from Niccolò MachiavelliHumanists and Essayists

  • Does a leader's private life matter if they do the job well?

    from ConfuciusConfucianism

  • Can you ever really explain why you like someone?

    from Michel de MontaigneHumanists and Essayists

  • What keeps a friendship going for years?

    from VoltaireEnlightenment Ethics

  • Have you ever felt at home in a place of worship that wasn't your own?

    from Ibn ArabiPersian and Sufi Poetry

  • Which is harder: winning against someone else, or winning against a bad habit?

    from Dhammapada 103Buddhist Philosophy

  • What small thing, done every day, would change your life most in a year?

    from Dhammapada 122Buddhist Philosophy

  • What are you putting off until “later” that you'd love to start now?

    from SenecaStoicism

  • Should a good friend tell you when you're wrong?

    from Friedrich NietzscheExistential Thought

  • What's one good thing from this week that we'd forget to mention?

    from Fyodor DostoevskyExistential Thought

  • Do you like the start of something, the middle, or the end best?

    from KenkōJapanese Aesthetics

  • Do you rest so you can work, or work so you can rest?

    from Rabindranath TagoreIndian Philosophy

  • Have you ever bought one new thing and then wanted to change everything around it?

    from Denis DiderotEnlightenment Ethics

  • Is beauty in the thing, or in the person looking at it?

    from David HumeEnlightenment Ethics

  • What's the last thing that made you laugh until it hurt?

    from Nicolas ChamfortHumanists and Essayists
